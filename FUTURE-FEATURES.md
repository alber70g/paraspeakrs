## Brainstorm 2026-11-09

Main problem to solve: steering the meeting while it runs. Meetings: in-room team
meetings and video calls.

New ideas picked:

- **Decisions and to-dos**: a live sidebar of decisions and action items (owner, date), exported at the end.
- **Catch me up**: a button for a summary of the last N minutes.
- **Suggested questions**: questions to ask now, only for me. Covered by a "Facilitator" persona in the persona library.
- **Final pass after the stop**: the full diarization replaces the live speakers; names given during the meeting are kept.
- **Summary and follow-up mail**: from the decisions and to-dos.
- **Search across meetings**: "what did we decide about X", also through the MCP server.

inline with a learned word list, participants from the calendar, enrolling a voice,
voice cache cleanup, starting `live follow` from the recorder, talk-time balance.

## Persona package: decisions

- Personas are stored as separate entities in a library and added to a meeting from that list, before or during the meeting. The library ships with a "Facilitator" preset whose comments are questions to ask.
- New personas during the meeting: a short questionnaire, like the intake, that proposes personas based on what was said so far. The model only proposes when asked (a button), never by itself.
- A persona added during the meeting either starts from now or reruns over the meeting so far. Rerun comments go into the timeline at the moment they are about.
- The sidebar lists every comment; clicking one scrolls the timeline to it.
- A persona can be paused or removed during the meeting.
- Personas added during the meeting are saved into the meeting's reusable brief.
- "Continue meetings" means resuming an interrupted meeting with its sections, comments and answers.
- The meeting overview becomes the home page of one web app that later replaces the TUI.
